# Genie Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive examples include:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Acknowledging mistakes and helping others learn
- Respecting the time and effort of others

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or events
- Hate speech or slurs targeting protected groups
- Publishing others' private information ("doxxing")
- Threats of violence or violent language
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, mailing lists, forums, chat, etc.)
- Discussions about the project outside official spaces
- Representations of the project in public spaces

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document it**: Note what happened, when, and who was involved
2. **Report privately**: Email governance@c2si.org with:
   - Description of the incident
   - People involved
   - Any relevant logs or screenshots
   - Your contact information

3. **Confidentiality**: Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed only by designated maintainers

### Investigation Process

1. **Acknowledgment**: You'll receive confirmation within 48 hours
2. **Assessment**: Maintainers will investigate the report
3. **Decision**: Appropriate action will be determined
4. **Follow-up**: You'll be informed of the outcome

### Consequences for Violations

Responses may include:
- **Warning**: First violation, maintainer notification
- **Temporary ban**: 30-day ban from community spaces
- **Permanent ban**: For severe or repeated violations
- **Legal action**: For illegal behavior

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was unjust, you may appeal by:
1. Emailing governance@c2si.org within 14 days
2. Providing additional context or evidence
3. Awaiting review by independent maintainers

## Special Circumstances

### Disagreements About Technical Decisions

Passionate disagreement about technical direction is normal and healthy. This is NOT a violation of this Code of Conduct. However:
- Maintain respectful tone
- Focus on ideas, not people
- Listen to other perspectives
- Seek consensus where possible
- Escalate disagreements respectfully if needed

### Mistakes and Learning

We value growth. If you make a mistake:
- Acknowledge it promptly
- Apologize sincerely
- Explain what you'll do differently
- Follow through

The community should accept apologies and move forward.
